# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FinTerminal is intended to be a technically serious, welcoming open-source project. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions focused on improving the software and its research workflows.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, reproducible technical feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- helping new contributors understand project conventions;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- protecting private data, credentials, and unpublished research materials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, or repeatedly ignoring maintainer instructions in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, release channels, and other spaces managed by the FinTerminal project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its contributors. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when practical, and focused on restoring a productive technical environment.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include credentials, private financial data, or unrelated sensitive information in a report.
